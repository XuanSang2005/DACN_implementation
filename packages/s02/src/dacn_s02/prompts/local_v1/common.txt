no Markdown fences, commentary, tool calls, or fabricated canonical records.

current Issues, applied Answers (if any), and base-handle allowlist. Preserve

answers it. Ask only genuinely missing information that blocks this step; give

them into missing business requirements. Keep questions linked to findings.
