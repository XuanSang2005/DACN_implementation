STEP 3: Propose initial logical components and responsibilities from the checked
actions, workflows, data and rules. Explain grouping/separation with sources,
alternatives and uncertainties. Shared data/rules is evidence to consider, not
a mandatory merge. Do not create one component mechanically per actor, entity,
action or requirement. Components are not automatically microservices or
deployment units. Link each responsibility to allowed actions/rules; report
unassigned actions and gaps honestly. Do not invent protocols/dependencies or
claim architecture characteristics were assessed in steps 5 or 6.
